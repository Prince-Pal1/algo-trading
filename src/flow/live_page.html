width runs from the level into the side price penetrates: a short at 4000 width 100 is watched 4000–4100, a long at 4000 width 100 is watched 3900–4000. It is also the risk — price leaving the far side is the level failing, and that price is the invalidation published with every signal.
Edits write through to config/levels.toml — the file stays the source of truth, so what you add here survives a restart. marked is when the level was first written down; it is preserved through edits, because a level you moved is still a level you marked then.
Advisory only — this never places an order. Evidence is shown for and against; read both before acting. A signal needs the sequence: ABSORBING (aggression failing) then TURNING (it reverses). Absorption on its own does not fire.
