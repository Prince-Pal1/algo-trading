### Holdout-window result (INCONCLUSIVE — not "fail")

The 21-day holdout produced **0 trades** across all 36 cells. Root cause: the deep_backtest engine consumes 250 bars as warmup before strategy signals are eligible to fire. With 21 days × ~17 bars/day = ~360 bars, warmup eats 250 bars (≈10 days), leaving only ~6.5 days of active evaluation. At the strategy's natural cadence (~1 trade per 23 days observed in tune-window matrix), the expected trade count over 6.5 days is **~0.3** — consistent with the observed zero.

This is an **infrastructure limitation** of the holdout window, not a strategy verdict. The holdout PF gate (1.2) is unmeetable when n_trades = 0.

### A1 verdict: DEFERRED

- **Tune pass** (Calmar 6.53 OOS via 5-fold WF retune): the strategy class has a viable parameter regime on historical XAUUSD.
- **Holdout pass**: cannot be evaluated with current data extent. Not a kill.
- **Live-track-record gap**: live PF 0.11 over 39 trades / 8 days ≠ tune Calmar 6.53. The 5.4× over-trading seen live is inconsistent with the tune cadence (1 trade/23d). Either:
  - **Hypothesis A**: live's `momentum_threshold=0.0` (no gate) over-trades vs the WF-retune-chosen stricter params (could be 0.005 or 0.01).
  - **Hypothesis B**: live runs continuously past warmup and gets to live signals immediately; the 5m feed (not just 1h) may add micro-noise.
  - **Hypothesis C**: gold's recent-month tick-noise regime is hostile to momentum strategies, and the WF folds 1, 3, 4 caught friendly regimes while live caught a hostile one.
- **Re-enable criterion (refined)**: extend the holdout window to ≥45 days (need 3+ weeks of additional data) AND apply the WF-chosen params, NOT live's defaults. Until then, leave disabled.

### Per-fold best params (extracted from tune)

The WF retune output stored per-fold `best_params` was None for all 5 folds (deep_backtest's `_wf_retune_on_train` skipped grid points with <5 trades on the 90-day train window). This is a known limitation: when the strategy's natural cadence is slow relative to the train window, retune can't differentiate cells.

Recommended next iteration (NEXT SPRINT, not this one): manually run the matrix phase on each grid cell over the full tune window (no per-fold retune; just compare 36 single-window backtests) to identify which params produced the WF Calmar 6.53.

### A2 diagnostic verdict: PASS

The HFM gate is "5/5 live trades have matching strategy emission" — confirmed. The strategy CODE agrees with the LIVE engine on every actual fill. The 14× live-vs-original-backtest signal-rate discrepancy (the HFM's feed-bug suspicion) is **NOT a feed bug** — it's a regime difference between the 4.3-year original backtest (1 trade per 25 days) and the 8.5-day live window (1 trade per 1.7 days). Funding-rate volatility was simply higher during the live window than the historical average.

### The 2 STRAT_ONLY events (worth investigating)

Between 04-17 and 04-23, the strategy code would have:
1. Exited on 04-18 08:00 (3 consecutive negative-funding bars triggered the kill)
2. Re-entered on 04-22 08:00 (cooldown elapsed + funding back to positive)
3. Exited on 04-23 08:00 (matched live)

Live held through both. Possible reasons:
- **Cooldown-state desync**: live engine's `_bars_since_exit` counter didn't reach the entry-eligibility threshold at the right time
- **Position-management latency**: live engine's signal→fill pipeline introduces delay; an exit signal at 04-18 might not have produced a fill before the entry signal at 04-22 fired, leading the engine to NOT execute the round-trip
- **Live `FundingSyntheticFeed` value drift**: live polls `/fapi/v1/premiumIndex` at 5-min cadence and emits at the 8h boundary; the value emitted may differ slightly from the historical `/fapi/v1/fundingRate` snapshot, possibly enough to flip a single negative-funding bar to neutral

Of these, **(c) is the most plausible** and aligns with HFM's hypothesis about feed-source drift. NOT a hard feed bug (because the 5/5 live trades all match), but a soft drift that causes occasional decision divergence on edge cases.
